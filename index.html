Eight Bells

Carlos Elena-Lenz

It is with great sorrow that we announce our good friend and Encinal Yacht Club member, Carlos Elena-Lenz, passed away Tuesday, November 3rd, with his wife Jaclyn and sister Sophia at his side.

Carlos started sailing competitively as a youngster in Florida in the Optimist and then Laser 2 classes. Among his numerous sailing achievements, Carlos sailed competitively in college (Tufts, ‘00), and was on the 2002 team that won the Hinman trophy for the US Team Racing Championship. This experience solidified his impact on the sport at a young age and helped usher in the era of Team Racing. Those of us who enjoyed his sailing acumen were always fortunate when he obliged a request to be in the boat as you could count on it being a good ride with him aboard.

When he wasn’t fire dancing, or philosophizing about trends in technology, Carlos enjoyed cruising with his family aboard their Corsair F27 Trimaran “Speed Spirit.”

Carlos is survived by his wife, Jaclyn, children Sabastián and Saphira (both on the EYC jr race team), sister Sophia, and parents Ana and Federico.

Fair winds, our friend. Your heart of gold and relentless positive outlook on life will be our guiding light.

If you are so inclined, please consider a donation in memory of Carlos to the family at https://www.supportnow.org/the-elena-lenz-family
